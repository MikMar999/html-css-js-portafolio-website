The Division of Labour

Poster design for The Division of Labour (2022)

A comedic skit about two people moving in together. Through playful back-and-forths, one character manipulates the other into carrying everything inside. As the absurdity escalates, the main character grows dizzy from the gaslighting—questioning whether he is even still on Earth.

  • Image 1: Side view of the costume: white sleep top, off-white fitted pants, and black shoes.
  • Image 2: Back view, highlighting the open-back design as the performer moves through the set.
